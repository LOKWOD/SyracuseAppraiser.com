LOCAL MARKET ANALYSIS

Mailing address and municipality are not the same thing.

A Liverpool mailing address does not by itself identify the municipality or market. Properties carrying the Liverpool postal name may be located in the Village of Liverpool, Town of Salina, Town of Clay or other nearby jurisdictions. Appraisal analysis should use the actual legal/municipal location and the market where buyers compete.

Correct identification of municipality, taxing jurisdiction and neighborhood is important for both credible analysis and accurate reporting.

Property characteristics we consider

  • actual municipality versus mailing address
  • school district
  • village versus suburban neighborhood
  • age and style of housing
  • lot utility and traffic influence
  • proximity to major employment and retail corridors
