LOCAL MARKET ANALYSIS

Postal name is not a substitute for actual jurisdiction.

A North Syracuse postal address may represent the village or properties in surrounding municipalities such as Clay, Cicero or Salina. The actual municipality, school district and neighborhood should guide comparable selection and reporting.

Correct geographic identification helps avoid mixing sales from distinct taxing jurisdictions or competitive submarkets simply because they share a mailing name.

Property characteristics we consider

  • actual municipality versus postal address
  • school district
  • village versus suburban subdivision
  • housing age and style
  • lot size and utility
  • traffic and commercial influences
